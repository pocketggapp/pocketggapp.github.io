Reporting a problem

When reporting an issue, please include as much of the following as you can:

  • What you were doing and what you expected to happen
  • What actually happened (screenshots or screen recordings if possible)
  • The tournament, event, or profile involved, and/or the start.gg link
  • Your iPhone model, iOS version, and pocketgg version

Common questions

I can't log in or my session expired

pocketgg signs you in through start.gg's own login page. If it fails, try again and make sure you finish the authorization on the presented start.gg login page. If the problem continues, go to your device's Safari settings, clear all cookies & website data, and try logging in again.

Tournament or bracket information looks wrong or is missing

pocketgg displays data provided by start.gg, so brackets, results, and event details are only as current as what start.gg returns. If something looks incorrect, check the tournament on start.gg first. Tournament organizers can fix their own event data there. If start.gg shows something different from pocketgg, email me with a link to the tournament and the issue you're seeing.

Where does pocketgg store my data?

Your information stays on your device. See the Privacy Policy for full details.

Is pocketgg affiliated with start.gg?

No. pocketgg is an independent app that uses start.gg's public API. For issues with your start.gg account itself, such as account recovery, please contact start.gg support.
